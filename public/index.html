01 · The Constraint

The constraint has moved from demand to delivery.

Electricity demand is growing again in most large economies. The limit is no longer the idea or the demand. It is delivery: the grid connection, the transformer, the turbine slot, the permit. The value in power now sits with those who can deliver.

02 · The Rule

After the physics works. Before a bank will lend. And after that.

We invest where the shortage is physical and can be checked, where the buyer can pay for the full length of the contract, and where the public and the government agree the project is needed. We pay for a shortage we have verified. We do not pay for one everybody already knows about, because that price is already high.

03 · Three Groups, One Firm

One firm for the whole life of a power technology.

Power companies need different capital at different ages. A first commercial unit, a growing fleet and a contracted asset do not share an instrument, a hold period or an investor. So Inertia Fund invests through three groups. Each has its own investment committee. All three share one partnership, one research desk and one bench of experts.

Early Inertia Seed to Series A

For companies whose science is proven and whose next step is the first commercial unit.

The one question

Can a first commercial unit be built on a date we can check?

Accelerated Inertia Flagship First unit to scale

For companies whose first unit is sold or contracted and whose remaining work is scale: more customers, more sites, more markets.

The one question

Can this product be sold, made and financed again, at a cost and contract form that scale?

We bring what a venture fund cannot: a buyer willing to be next, the work that makes a fleet bankable, and the financing that pays for deployment.

Consolidated Inertia Mature assets

For mature power assets and companies, where the return comes from structuring, delivery and operating judgment rather than from technology.

The one question

Can this asset be capitalized better than its current owner capitalizes it?

We work as a specialist co-investor beside larger platforms.

05 · The Handoff

A company moves between groups only when it has earned it.

The earlier question must be answered in writing: a unit running at a paying customer and measured independently, or a contract a lender or an insurer will price. And the capital it now needs must be the next group's instrument. When one of our groups invests beside another, a third party sets the price, investor committees on both sides consent, and the arrangement is disclosed before first close.

06 · Commercial Access

A proven product can still wait years for a utility to be the first buyer.

We are building a program of utilities, grid operators and industrial partners that review our companies on a fixed timetable. We are also building a delivery and bankability team that handles certification, warranties and insurance, so that a first fleet can be financed.
