Accelerated Inertia

Can this already-sold product be sold, made and financed again? Accelerated Inertia backs growth capital for products that already work and have a paying customer. We invest when the remaining risk is selling, making and financing the product again.

Purpose

Can this already-sold product be sold, made and financed again?

The power system needs more capacity, faster than it can be built. Many products that help already work and already have a paying customer. What they lack is the second, tenth and hundredth order: a buyer who trusts them, a factory that can make them, and money to deploy them.

Accelerated Inertia backs that step with growth capital and with people who have built and run power systems. We invest when the remaining risk is selling, making and financing the product again, because that is the risk we know how to manage.

Methodology v1.2

Draft, 3 Oct 2026, pending investment committee review

Entry Gates A1–A9

  • Sold and measured
  • Money buys repetition
  • Repeatable product
  • Delivery risk not physics
  • Improvable
  • Purpose fit
  • Size and role fit: rounds of about $30–150m and our check of $20–75m as lead or co-lead
  • Counterparty
  • No open kill

Repeatability Scorecard

Eight axes scored 0–5:

AxisWeight (%)
D — Sold again20
U — Unit economics and path to profit16
M — Made again13
F — Financed again13
I — Improves again12
R — Regime portability9
T — Team, incentives and governance9
P — Price and return path8

Composite = 20 × (0.20 D + 0.16 U + 0.13 M + 0.13 F + 0.12 I + 0.09 R + 0.09 T + 0.08 P)

All 3s = 60. Unknown scores at most 2.

Floors by Stage

  • DIVE needs D and U at 2 or more
  • IC1 and IC2 need D, U and P at 3 or more

Screening Actions

  • STOP: A gate fails, a kill, D or U below 2, or composite below 45
  • WATCH: 45–63.9
  • WATCH-PRICE: DIVE-quality but P below 2
  • DIVE: 64 or more with at least two axes at 4

Not-available Rule

An axis score can be blank ("Not available"), meaning it is not yet verified from evidence. A verified zero is different from an unverified blank. A score counts as Not available if the data is older than 24 months or is from before a change of control.

Not-available scores are excluded from the composite. A company is ranked once at least 6 of 8 axes, including Sold again (D) and Unit economics (U), are verified. Until then, it appears on the Needs data list, showing coverage as "n/8 verified" and sorted by verified-axis count.

Stress Overlay

Six shocks: rates, AI data-center bust, policy reversal, tariffs/China, commodity swing, grid reform works.

The stressed composite is the lowest of the six. Threshold is 55 at every stage after screening. A spread above 10 marks the company regime-dependent.

Deal Fit

Reported beside the score and never blended into it:

  • L — Our leverage (scored by a partner who is not the sponsor)
  • P — Price
  • Portfolio fit

Flags

  • Discretionary vs compliance-mandated subsidy share
  • Runway under 18 months
  • Competition (no written "why this one")
  • Alignment

Return Bar (proposed)

3x by year five or 4x by year seven, 22% gross base case, bear case returns capital, credible 5x upside.
